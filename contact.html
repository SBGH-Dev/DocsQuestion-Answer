Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get an OpenAI API key?
Visit platform.openai.com/api-keys and create a free account. Click "Create new secret key" and copy the key (starts with "sk-"). New accounts receive $5 in free credits.
Is my API key secure?
Yes! Your API key is stored only in your browser's localStorage and never sent to our servers. All API calls go directly from your browser to OpenAI. We have no access to your key or your documents.
How much does it cost to use?
You only pay OpenAI for the API usage. GPT-4o-mini costs approximately $0.005-0.01 per question. A $5 credit balance can handle 500+ questions. There are no fees from us - the app is completely free to use.
What types of PDFs are supported?
We support all standard PDF files with text content. Scanned PDFs (images) are not supported unless they have OCR text embedded. The app works best with text-based PDFs like research papers, reports, articles, and documents.
Can I use this offline?
No, an internet connection is required to communicate with OpenAI's API. However, your documents are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded to any server except OpenAI for generating answers.
